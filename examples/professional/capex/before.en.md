# Warehouse automation investment review

Fictional scenario. Prepared for the operations director and investment committee. All figures are pre-investment assumptions, not measured equipment performance. Money is in constant KRW million unless stated otherwise.

Four comparable zones each process 1,200 eligible cases daily for 300 operating days annually, with fixed demand and no sales uplift. Of eight scheduled hours daily, 75%, or six hours, are occupied processing time. A two-worker team processes 200 cases per occupied team-hour manually. Automation targets an unvalidated 300 with the same staffing and case mix. Processing would take four rather than six team-hours, releasing four worker-hours daily per zone, or 1,200 annually and 4,800 sitewide. Scheduled time, occupied team-hours and worker-hours are different measures.

Cash realization assumes cancellation of 600 agency hours per zone annually at KRW 30,000 per invoiced hour. This yields 18 per zone and cancels only 2,400 sitewide hours, not all released hours. Permanent payroll stays unchanged. Other released hours support redeployment without cash savings. Spend cancellation remains unvalidated. Annual gross cash savings are 18 for the pilot and 72 sitewide. Incremental maintenance, software and energy cost 4 and 12 respectively, leaving net savings of 14 and 60.

Immediate four-zone deployment costs 240 upfront. A one-zone pilot lasts one operating year and costs 75 upfront. Conditional expansion reuses pilot equipment but costs another 190, totaling 265. Spending and go-live coincide. The model assumes no ramp, constant savings and uniform cash accrual, with expansion immediately after year one. It excludes tax, depreciation, financing, discounting, working capital and salvage. Simple payback is 240 / 60 = 4.00 years for full deployment. Continuing the pilot without expansion gives 75 / 14, about 5.36 years. Conditional staged payback from initial outlay is 1 + (75 - 14 + 190) / 60, about 5.18 years. Year one earns only 14.

Expansion would require target throughput on comparable case mix, a case-error rate at most 0.5%, no unresolved critical safety findings and documented cancellation of 600 agency hours during the pilot year. Finance and operations must substantiate the remaining zones' economics before separate expansion approval. Management recommends authorizing only the 75 pilot tranche because throughput and spend cancellation remain unvalidated. Neither tranche is approved or completed. The pilot commits 165 less initially, but staging adds 25 in capital and delays modeled payback. Immediate deployment has better assumed economics but commits more capital before cash realization is proven.
