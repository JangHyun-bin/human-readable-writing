# Policy source packet

## Topic, reader, and purpose

This is an original transport-policy evaluation memo for a professional reviewer deciding what evidence to request about New York congestion pricing. Its bounded question is what page 13 of MTA's December 17, 2025 board presentation reports about vehicle entries, car speeds, and PM2.5, and what the slide does not establish. The case ID is `policy`.

The drafts are project-authored first drafts, not excerpts from MTA and not native skill outputs. Their evidence request is the original memo author's stated proposal. MTA is not credited with that proposal. Neither draft recommends repeal, expansion, replication elsewhere, or a decision on the program's overall merits. The implemented program is not described as a trial.

## Metric and condition checks

- Entries retain the CBD geography and the daily unit for 71,000. Neither a unique-vehicle population nor vehicle-miles can be recovered from entry counts. Dividing a rounded daily reduction by a rounded percentage would not establish an exact baseline. No calculated baseline is supplied.
- The entry observation period, comparison period, and baseline construction remain unknown from page 13. They do not inherit the PM2.5 first-six-month window or a full-year window from the presentation title.
- Speed retains the CBD geography and the 4.3% increase. A percentage change in speed is not the same percentage change in travel time. The draft does not introduce an absolute speed, fixed trip distance, weighting rule, observation period, comparison period, hours, baseline, or measurement provider.
- PM2.5 retains MTA's 22% and attributed first-six-month wording. The slide does not state the exact study dates, concentration statistic, percentage denominator, or detailed study geography. The memo does not substitute a prior-year denominator or a modeled denominator. It requests those definitions.
- The slide's zone heading does not license a citywide conclusion or establish that CBD and the study's geographic sample are identical. No tolling map or road-exclusion list is supplied.
- No percentage is changed to percentage points. Concentration is not emissions, all pollution, or individual exposure. The agency summary is not recast as an independent causal evaluation.
- P07 and P08 are identified as `author_judgment`. The requested independent review has not been performed by this packet, and the request does not express a repeal or expansion decision.

These are background leads from planning, not sources used to establish additional results in this packet. The earlier plan raised date, study-window, and correction questions. This production packet does not claim to resolve those questions or repeat a correction date or correction-content claim. It deliberately preserves the board slide's attribution and its unspecified details instead. Resolving the underlying study is unnecessary for a memo that requests that evidence rather than endorses its causal result.

The unselected material includes modeled concentration differences and denominators, monitor counts, monitor-day counts, regional results, exact study dates, model limitations, and correction details. None is a hidden preservation requirement for these drafts. No health, equity, displacement, long-term persistence, revenue, ridership, or net-welfare conclusion is made. Page 13's separate noise-complaint and visit claims are also outside the selected question.
